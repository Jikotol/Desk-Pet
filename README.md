# Desk Pet

A Desk Pet is a physical electronic cat made to simulate the emotional connection and responsibility associated with taking care of real a cat. Throughout its life, the user(its owner) feeds and plays with the cat to keep it happy and alive. It features 2 buttons for the play and feed functions, a i2C LCD 1602 for custom facial expressions, and a RGB LED to represent the cats mood. 

The Desk Pet was made for my passion project in Advanced Manufacturing, sophomore year. It was made in 6 weeks, with limited Arduino programming experience, from April to May 2026.
